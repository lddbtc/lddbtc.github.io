Why hire an independent inspector — not the factory's

The single most important decision in sourcing from China is who pays the inspector. If the factory pays, you are not the client.

Home › Why independent

The conflict of interest nobody mentions

On Alibaba, Made-in-China and Global Sources, a large share of "manufacturers" are trading companies. They quote factory prices, show showroom photos, then quietly subcontract your order to whoever is cheapest that month. The "inspector" they offer you is often someone they pay — and the report is written for the supplier, not for you.

An independent inspector removes that conflict completely: I am paid by you, and only you. No supplier commission, no relationship with the factory, no reason to soften a verdict. If the plant is fake, I say it is fake.

What a supplier-recommended inspection actually costs you

🟡 Softened reports

A supplier-paid inspector has a reason to downplay defects: the next job comes from the same factory. Minor issues become "acceptable"; major ones become "easy to fix".

🟡 Trading-front blind spot

They verify the address you were given — which may be a rented office. They rarely trace it back to the real OEM, so you never learn you are paying an intermediary.

🟡 Deposit already gone

By the time a friendly report reaches you, your deposit is wired. A $300 inspection after the fact cannot undo a $30,000 mistake.

How an independent engagement works

  1. You send the supplier — company name, factory address, product and what to check. Email or WeChat, both work.
  2. I quote one all-in USD price — the earliest on-site date, no hidden extras. You approve before anything moves.
  3. You pay me directly — via a Payoneer request or PayPal invoice (USD only). Never the supplier, never a bank wire you can't reverse.
  4. I visit and report to you — timestamped photos, video and a straight English report within 24–48h, before you release the balance.

When independence matters most

  • First order with a new supplier — the exact moment deposit risk is highest.
  • High-value or certified goods — machinery, food contact, PPE, building materials where a fake cert is catastrophic.
  • "Manufacturer" with no visible plant — most trading fronts look identical until someone stands at the gate.
