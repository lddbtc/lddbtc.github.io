Frequently asked questions

The questions importers ask before they book an independent inspection in China.

Home › FAQ
Do you take commission from suppliers?
No. Never. That single fact is why my verdict is worth reading — I am paid by you and only by you.
How fast can you visit a factory?
For suppliers in Shandong, usually within 48 hours of booking. Elsewhere in China, typically 3–5 days including travel.
How do I pay?
USD only — I send a Payoneer payment request or a PayPal invoice with your quote (no bank wire needed). A small deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due on report delivery. All agreed in writing first.
What if the factory turns out to be fake?
That is the most valuable money you will spend. You get documented evidence and avoid wiring money to a reseller. The audit verdict states real plant, limited, or reseller / front.
Which industries do you cover?
My home ground is the Weifang, Shandong manufacturing belt: aluminium / windows / curtain wall (Linqu), waterproofing membranes & pipes (Shouguang), safety gloves / doors / tyre moulds (Gaomi), construction machinery & livestock climate control (Qingzhou), food (Zhucheng / Changle), pet food (Changyi) and environmental equipment (Anqiu) — all same-day, no travel surcharge. Elsewhere in China I travel at cost.
What does the report contain?
Original timestamped photos and video taken on site, the honest verdict (including "walk away"), clear English formatted to forward to your team or bank, and a direct line to the person who was on the floor.
