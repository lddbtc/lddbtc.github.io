Who I am

I'm Dongdong Liu, based in Weifang, Shandong. I've spent 17 years in the building-materials and home-supply chain and in factory acceptance work — the kind of background that recognises a running extrusion line from a staged photo, and a real plant from a rented office.

Why independence, not just inspection

I do not take a cent from suppliers. No commission, no referral fee, no relationship with the factory whose gate I'm standing at. That is the entire point: when the right call is "walk away", I have no reason to soften it. The report you forward to your team or your bank is written for you.

What I do, plainly

  • Factory audits — is this a real plant or a trading front? Written verdict.
  • Pre-shipment inspection — AQL sampling, function, workmanship, packing, on the finished lot.
  • Loading supervision — watch the container close, record the seal, confirm the right goods go in.
  • During-production checks — catch defects while they are still cheap to fix.

How I work with you

  1. You send the supplier, product and what to check.
  2. I reply with one firm all-in USD quote and the earliest on-site date.
  3. You pay me directly (Payoneer or PayPal, USD only) — never the supplier.
  4. You get timestamped photos, video and a straight English report within 24–48h.
